# Responsive QA Checklist

Viewport targets:

- small mobile: 360px wide
- large mobile: 390px to 430px wide
- tablet: 768px wide
- laptop: 1280px wide
- desktop: 1440px and above

Check:

- navigation and header do not wrap awkwardly
- primary CTAs remain visible
- forms are usable with touch
- tables become cards, scroll regions, or prioritized columns
- modals fit the viewport and keep destructive actions visible
- popovers do not escape screen edges
- status badges remain readable
- content does not overlap after dynamic data changes

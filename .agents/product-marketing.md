# Mandate402 Product Marketing Context

## Product

Mandate402 is a treasury-control and governance layer for x402 machine commerce on Morph.

It helps teams let AI agents spend money safely by enforcing:

- approved vendor allowlists
- budget caps
- mandate expiry windows
- receipt requirements
- operator-visible audit trails
- explicit handling for `execution_unknown` payment outcomes

## What the product is not

- not the payment facilitator
- not the vendor marketplace itself
- not a generic AI agent dashboard
- not a consumer wallet or neobank

## Primary audiences

- AI-native startup founders
- platform and operator teams
- treasury and finance approvers
- security and compliance reviewers
- engineers integrating x402-paid vendor workflows

## Core conversions

- operator sign-in to the product
- design-partner or implementation conversations
- trust-building review by technical buyers and approvers

## Public pages in scope today

- `/` homepage
- `/policies`
- `/terms`

## Positioning

Mandate402 sells control over machine spend, not payment settlement itself.

Key framing:

- controlled spend for autonomous agents
- treasury command for x402 agentic commerce
- operator-visible policy and audit enforcement
- Morph-native mandate lifecycle and governance surface

## Priority topic areas

- AI agent payments
- x402 payments
- Morph ecosystem
- treasury controls for AI agents
- governed machine commerce
- policy-based agent spending
- operator auditability for AI spend

## Messaging constraints

- keep wording concrete and technical
- do not promise rankings, traffic, or compliance outcomes
- do not invent enterprise features not present in the repo
- avoid generic startup marketing language
